## 📝 NAMING TIPS

### What Makes a Good Platform Name:

1. **Short:** 2-3 syllables ideal
2. **Clear:** Purpose obvious or easily explained
3. **Unique:** Stand out from competitors
4. **Spell-able:** Easy to spell after hearing once
5. **Global:** Works in multiple languages
6. **Positive:** Creates good feelings
7. **Brandable:** Can build identity around it
8. **Domain Available:** .com or .io available
9. **Social Media:** Handle available
10. **Trademark:** Not already trademarked

### Red Flags to Avoid:

❌ Too similar to competitors
❌ Hard to spell or pronounce
❌ Negative connotations in other languages
❌ Too generic (can't trademark)
❌ Too trendy (will date quickly)
❌ Domain not available
❌ Trademarked by others

## 🚀 NEXT STEPS

1. **Check Domain Availability:**

   - Visit namecheap.com or godaddy.com
   - Check .com, .io, and .app domains
   - Reserve your favorite immediately

2. **Check Social Media Handles:**

   - Instagram: @yourbrandname
   - Facebook: /yourbrandname
   - Twitter: @yourbrandname
   - TikTok: @yourbrandname

3. **Trademark Search:**

   - USPTO.gov (USA)
   - Check international trademarks
   - Ensure no conflicts

4. **Get Feedback:**

   - Ask 10-20 teachers
   - Ask students
   - Check pronunciation in target languages

5. **Make Decision:**
   - Choose primary and backup
   - Register domain immediately
   - Create basic landing page
